# Downtown 6-train exit

Native 1536 × 1024 transparent station portal, with a lossless runtime WebP.
This revised image replaces the rejected rectangular station set. It uses an
asymmetric iron canopy, angled cheek-wall, tapered stone approach, contact
shadow and a shallow descent to a recessed train doorway. The green 6 and
railing sign are painted; the destination display is lettered at runtime.

This is stylised station scenery, not an engineering reconstruction. The street
apron is at native y=750; the sill at y=807. The runner and contact shadow
follow the registered approach in finish-gates.js down 27.36 game pixels. The
near jamb occludes the runner down to shoe height. Camera and city art remain
unchanged. The right side continues beyond the viewport; the left approach
tapers into the existing street. The existing native stone foundation is continued
below the apron, blended over its first 240 native pixels. No global
transparency/fade or floating slab.
