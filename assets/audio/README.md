# Title music

`title-mellow-lounge.mp3` is the user's supplied recording, copied byte for byte.
Display title: **Why talking to people is a superpower x Mellow Lounge**.

It loops on the title screen through the existing music mixer. Its gain is 0.72
relative to the chapter track (about 2.9 dB softer), with a 500 ms fade-in.
The music slider and mute preference still apply. A level starts only after
pausing the title track; Contrarian retains its exclusive soundtrack.
Browsers that block audible autoplay begin playback on the first menu input.
