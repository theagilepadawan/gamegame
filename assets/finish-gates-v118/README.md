# Chapter entrances — native art

Three 1536 × 1024 transparent painted masters and lossless runtime WebPs.
Original game-specific artwork generated with image_gen against the approved
Downtown, Midtown and Park paintings. No new scenery is baked around the gates.
The classical-gate typography is supplied in the renderer; the accurate 505
lettering is part of the blue-glass architecture. The Midtown replacement uses
the approved assets/venue-details-v111/fifth.webp as its architectural reference. Runtime registration, doorway boundaries,
foreground pier and floor baseline are in `scripts/finish-gates.js`.
The full image is the back layer; a registered crop of the near pier occludes
the runner. No resize, sharpening or artificial upscaling is applied to assets.
See `docs/finish-and-launch-v118.md` for research and architectural direction.

Every exit is placed at the far right. The existing office host is rendered
separately beside 505, retaining her original portrait and gentle wrist greeting.
