# Version 1.20 — Portrait and story-transition polish

See [the current verification record](verification-v120.md) for this release.
The [version 1.19 record](verification-v119.md) covers the retained film and
finish structures; [version 1.19.2](verification-v1192.md) covers title autoplay.

The opening call uses the approved sketch-style Grete portrait and a Nicolai
portrait refined from the supplied photographs, within a minimal two-face
layout. Local speech and blink patches hold each head and body still. The
approved title thumbs-up follows the call. Downtown ends at the recessed 6
train, whose destination board reads “Union Square,” aligned with its perspective.

Midtown completion opens results immediately. Only “Next: Central Park” plays
the cropped user-supplied gift film, with the original quiet piano cue “A change
of pace.” Its actual ending or Skip starts Central Park. Direct map selection
of Central Park bypasses the film.

Title music attempts autoplay directly. When the browser requires a gesture,
the first ordinary click, touch release or keypress starts it. The separate
music-start button is removed. Suspended audio is not reported as audible.

The current city paintings, camera, remaining Easter-egg positions, gameplay
sprites, Story/Endless progression and original source assets are retained.
The previous release evidence is in [version 1.18](verification-v118.md).
