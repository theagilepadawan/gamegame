# Endless difficulty progression — version 1.27

The HUD level, procedural course pressure and music use the same pace marks:
1.5×, 2×, 2.5×, 3×, then a new tier every additional 0.5×. The original music
pitch steps still land on the next musical bar and stop increasing after 3×;
course tiers continue. Old 32-second art/theme changes no longer announce a
false difficulty promotion. IPO arena time does not advance street tiers.

Every tier increases the share of skill sections and tightens their landing
windows. Combinations grow from three to six actions; mandatory slide sections,
ordinary gaps, platforms, rare events and real recovery remain in circulation.
The 340 + 3t street speed from 1.26 is retained, without late-run easing.

Four new seeded families extend the course to 22 types:

- Rising Estimates: required jumps across raised islands in a continuous pit.
- Double Exposure (1.5×): a longer opening with a raised double-jump landing.
- Volatility Stairs (2×): moving and crumbling landings at varied heights.
- Compound Interest (2.5×): two marked setup jumps, then a high optional
  triple-jump dividend shelf. Missing the chain leaves a playable lower route.

Flight time, landing width, elevation and platform motion vary independently
within the family's constraints. Landings derive from the actual jump impulses
and gravity. Far-bank exits add real intermediate steps when a random hill
would otherwise be too high, and the final shelf connects into solid ground.
A deep pit's fall limit follows its visible banks and platforms, rather than
an invisible original ground line through the middle of the opening.

Small take-off cues preview the action when the landing is beyond the viewport.
Coin arcs, numbered setup decks and the marked upper shelf use the existing
financial platform treatment. Native city art, player sprites, Story courses,
entrance fade and music recordings retain their existing bytes and behavior.
